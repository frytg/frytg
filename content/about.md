# About me

I'm **Daniel Freytag** — or _Dan_ for short — a DevOps and backend engineer based in Ehrenkirchen near Freiburg in southwest Germany.

## Professional background

For **7+ years at [SWR Audio Lab](https://www.swr.de/unternehmen/audiolab/index.html)**, I have been building and running cloud and backend infrastructure supporting public-broadcasting platforms like [ARD Sounds](https://www.ardsounds.de/) and SWR's radio apps. That work spans real-time radio metadata distribution ([ARD Eventhub](https://github.com/swrlab/ard-eventhub)), voice-assistant integrations, audio delivery pipelines, and high-availability APIs serving **billions of requests per month**. More recently, I've also been working on the [responsible integration](https://www.swr.de/unternehmen/ki-guidelines-100.pdf) of [AI into public broadcasting](https://www.ard.de/die-ard/presse-kontakt/ard-pressemeldungen/2026/02-25-ard-popwellen-fuehren-regionalisierten-ki-service-fuer-wetter-und-verkehr-ein-100.html).

Before focusing full-time on backend and cloud engineering, I worked across **live and film production**, **press photography**, and **web development** — building everything from interactive messenger chatbots nominated for the [Deutscher Radiopreis 2019](https://www.swr3.de/wir/deutscher-radiopreis-2019-swr3-dreimal-nominiert-100.html) to high-traffic pandemic information platforms and virtual trade fairs. Having stood in broadcast control rooms and behind cameras myself shapes how I build infrastructure today: live systems don't get a second take.

Alongside my role at SWR, I take on selected **freelance engineering and consulting projects** ([`/services/`](/services/)) for teams that need practical help with Cloud & DevOps, EU-sovereign cloud migrations, audio/broadcast backends, or privacy-first AI.

## Values & how I build

- **Systems built to last beyond my involvement**: Infrastructure should be reproducible from a clean git checkout, documented in plain Markdown RFCs, and operable by the next engineer on call.
- **Always touch a running system**: Software that is never updated eventually becomes a security and reliability liability. Small, regular updates beat multi-year rescue rewrites.
- **European digital sovereignty**: Whenever practical, I prefer and recommend European cloud, CDN, and DNS providers with clear GDPR compliance and transparent pricing.
- **Quality over quantity**: Fewer dependencies, strict types, observable failure modes, and calm, direct communication — reflected in our public [SWR Audio Lab Engineering Principles](https://github.com/swrlab/swrlab/blob/main/PRINCIPLES.md).
