# SEO summaries

Read the post. Draft five `seo_description` options. Do not edit the file. Do not return YAML. A bullet list, nothing else.

Match the post's case, person, and rhythm. One hook, not a synopsis. 140 characters max. No emoji, hashtags, or "this post" / "discover" / "learn how". Do not invent facts. Do not reuse the current `seo_description` or `summary`.

Shorter is fine. Do not pad toward 140.

## Examples

Lowercase, first person, short sentences:

> the estimate was for the feature. nobody priced the three weeks of waiting on another team. the pull request got faster. the meeting did not.

- the estimate covered the feature. not the three weeks after.
- three weeks of waiting. the pull request was the easy part.
- the meeting did not get faster.
- nobody priced the waiting.
- the estimate was for the feature. that was the mistake.

Not this. Same facts, wrong voice, and it closes the argument:

- This post argues that estimates miss coordination delay, and faster code review did not shorten the meetings.

Sentence case, travel note, one scene:

> We landed after dark. The river was low. Someone had left a hammock under the big tree, and that was the whole plan.

- Landed after dark. The river was low.
- A hammock under the big tree, and that was the plan.
- The river was lower than the map said.
- After dark, with one hammock and no other plan.
- Someone had already picked the spot.

Not this. A topic list:

- A journey through night landings, low rivers, hammock camping, and the challenges of travel planning.

How-to, sentence case, still a teaser:

> Moving a repo off GitHub takes four commands if the remote is already public. The part that breaks is the old CI secret.

- Four commands, if the remote is already public.
- The move is easy. The old CI secret is not.
- How to move a public repo in four commands.
- The remote was the easy part.
- What breaks is the secret you forgot to rotate.

Weeknotes, lowercase, a question left open:

> cursor is moving. i have not decided if i am.

- cursor is moving. i have not decided if i am.
- still not sure what to do about cursor.
- the tool moved. i didn't.
- how are we feeling about this one?
- no decision yet. just the feeling.
